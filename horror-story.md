# The Knock at 3:17 AM

Every night at exactly 3:17 AM, someone knocked on Jake's apartment door.

But whenever he checked the hallway, nobody was there.

One night, Jake found a note under his door:

"Don't open the door tonight."

At 3:17 AM, the knocking started again.

Knock... Knock... Knock...

Then a voice whispered:

"Jake... I know you're home."

Jake checked his security camera.

The hallway was empty.

But the camera showed something impossible...

Jake was standing outside his own apartment.

The video version of Jake slowly turned toward the camera and smiled.

Then Jake heard another knock.

This time, it came from inside his bedroom.

A whisper followed:

"You should have listened."

The screen went black.

3:18 AM.

Jake was never alone.
